# US-007 Product / Catalog Management

**As** an authorized partner **I want** to find products by search and category **so that** I can see what I can sell.

Build: Commerce catalog and channel, product visibility by Account Group, partner site pages with Search Bar, Search Results, category facet and specification facets. No custom code unless a gap is found.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] AC1 Product search by name, SKU, description.
- [ ] AC2 Category-wise visibility: a partner sees only products in categories mapped to their account groups.
- [ ] AC3 Hierarchy: category tree navigation and breadcrumb (e.g. Networking › Routers › Branch and SMB).
- [ ] AC4 (Good to have) additional criteria as specification facets and on the product page.
- [ ] AC5 Account group assignment happens on activation from approved POS categories (US-005).

## Tests
- End to end with two partners in different groups: each sees only their categories.
