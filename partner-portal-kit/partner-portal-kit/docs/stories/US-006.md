# US-006 Partner Management Dashboard

**As** internal staff **I want** to search all partners and open their details.

Build: `partner-admin-ui` Partners view and detail view (reads objects + Account via user-agent OAuth).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] AC1 Search by company name, contact name, registration number, VAT ID (case-insensitive, partial).
- [ ] AC2 Filters: status, country, account manager, category.
- [ ] AC3 Table: company + VAT, primary contact, country, status chip, AM, partner since, POS count; sortable; 10/25/50 per page; CSV export of the current filter.
- [ ] AC4 Row click opens detail: Overview, Contacts, POS locations, Documents, Contract, Lifecycle history, Users; actions per role (deactivate, reactivate, terminate).
- [ ] AC5 Only data the viewer is permitted to see is returned (permission checks in Liferay, not just hidden in UI).

## Tests
- Component tests for search, filters, pagination.
- End to end: search a VAT ID, open detail, see history.
