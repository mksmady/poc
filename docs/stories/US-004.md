# US-004 Legal Approval Workflow

**As** legal **I want** to review AM-approved applications and record the contract **so that** only contracted partners get access.

Build: Kaleo `Legal Review` and `Contract Pending` tasks, `partner-admin-ui` legal queue, contract-signed endpoint.

Statuses: Invitation Sent, Registration In Progress, Submitted, Approval Pending, Revision Requested, Under Legal Review, Contract Pending, Authorized, Rejected, Terminated, Deactivated, Expired (see `status-lifecycle.md`).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] AC1 Auto-route: AM approval creates a legal task for role Partner Legal; any legal user can claim it.
- [ ] AC2 Dashboard: tabs Under legal review / Contract pending / Authorized this month / Rejected and terminated; columns company, country, AM, status, days in queue (over SLA highlighted), reviewer; Claim action.
- [ ] AC3 Approve: status `contractPending`; `PM_CONTRACT_PENDING`.
- [ ] AC4 Request revision: as US-003 AC4 with `revisionRequestedBy = legal`; resubmission returns to legal.
- [ ] AC5 Reject: reason required; status `rejected`; `PM_REJECTED`.
- [ ] AC6 Terminate: from `contractPending`, `authorized` or `deactivated`; reason required; status `terminated`; account and users inactive; `PM_TERMINATED` to partner, AM and admins.
- [ ] AC7 Contract signed: date, contract reference, signed PDF (stored as ApplicationDocument `signedContract`); sets `contractSigned = true`, status `authorized`, triggers US-005 activation.
- [ ] AC8 Terminated records: never deleted; company VAT and registration number added to the watchlist used by US-001 AC2 and US-002 AC9.

## Tests

- End to end: claim → approve → mark signed → partner receives activation email.
